# Rocks & Minerals 2027 Starter Pack: slide-by-slide audit

**Source:** `Rocks Minerals 2027 Starter Pack - cddb1659-….pdf`. The deck has 171 slides. Every slide is a single raster image, so the text was OCR'd and then checked against the rendered PNG.

**Authority used:** the official 2027 Division B Rules Manual, *Rocks and Minerals B* (pp. B41–B45, sections 2–4, and the 2027 National Rocks & Minerals List). The same rules are extracted in `docs/season_2027/manual_scope/rocks-and-minerals.md`. Rule references below use the manual's numbering: for example, 3.h.v means *Competition › Mineral Topics › habit*.

**Method:** every notes slide (1–62) was viewed as a PNG. Every exam page (63–171) was re-OCR'd from the embedded page image at 2× native resolution and parsed into questions and keys. All pages containing a figure, plus every page whose OCR looked garbled, were then read visually. Slides 153–171 were compared pixel by pixel with slides 105–123.

## 2. The pack's Official List vs the manual's list, specimen by specimen

The manual's list, counted by leaf name, has **84 minerals + 45 rocks = 129 names**. Group headings (Amphibole Group, Feldspar Group, Plagioclase feldspars, Potassium feldspars, Garnet Group, Mica Group, Pyroxene Group, Apatite Group, Quartz varieties, Coal/Limestone/Sandstone/Schist varieties) are not counted. The repo's "151" and "150/152" figures come from counting different things: `division_b_scope.json` `named_entities` also counts group names and the 10 gem varieties. I could not reproduce 151 exactly from the manual. The specimen-by-specimen result does not depend on the count.

**Result: nothing is missing, nothing extra, nothing misspelled, and every asterisk matches.** Every one of the 129 names appears once on slides 56–58, spelled as in the manual: Celestite/Celestine, Goethite/Limonite, Orthoclase/Microcline, Agate/Chalcedony, Tiger's Eye, Greywacke, Chert/Flint, Rock Salt (Halite), Rock Gypsum. All **27 starred minerals** (Bornite, Stibnite, Pyrolusite, Rutile, Zincite, Magnesite, Rhodochrosite, Siderite, Turquoise, Pyromorphite, Vanadinite, Actinolite, Tremolite, Apophyllite, Chrysocolla, Dioptase, Lepidolite, Prehnite, Rhodonite, Spodumene, Jasper, Smoky Quartz, Tiger's Eye, Stilbite, Willemite, Wollastonite, Zircon) and all **8 starred rocks** (Diatomite, Greywacke, Syenite, Tuff, Tuff Breccia, Amphibolite, Talc Schist/Soapstone, Serpentinite) match.

Differences in *presentation*, none of which change membership:

| # | Item | Manual | Pack | Impact |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Opal** | Listed under **Quartz varieties** | Moved to "Individual Silicates" (slide 58) | A student citing the List's family will disagree with the pack. The pack's placement is mineralogically defensible (opal is an amorphous mineraloid, not quartz), but it is not the List's. Teach both facts. |
| 2 | Asterisk legend | "*Specimens marked … are for State and National Tournaments" | Never stated on slides 54–62 (only in exam headers, e.g. slide 87) | Students may not know which items are regional-safe. |
| 3 | Footnote 1 | Bornite¹, Magnesite¹, Willemite¹, Diatomite¹, Syenite¹, Tuff¹, Tuff Breccia¹: "information will be provided such as diagnostic properties, chemical formulas, grain size, or composition" | Footnote marks dropped; slide 59 generalises it to all hard-to-see specimens | Loses the signal that these 7 will come with supplied data. |
| 4 | Variety qualifiers | Gypsum: Alabaster **(massive)**, Satin Spar **(fibrous)**, Selenite **(crystalline)**; Grossular **(green variety)**; Orthoclase/Microcline **(tan/pink variety)** | Qualifiers dropped on slides 57–58 (feldspar colour kept on slide 59) | Minor. The qualifiers are identification hints. |
| 5 | Family labels | Minerals grouped by anion class; silicates by group | Adds an invented "Individual Silicates" bucket | Harmless, but it is not List wording. |
| 6 | Limestone "Fossil" | "Fossil Limestone" | "Fossil" inside "Limestone (…)" | Harmless. |

**Draft vs final:** the pack says it was built from the *draft* manual. Its list matches the final manual's list exactly, so no list-membership drift is visible. In event parameters, the only drift is the omission of rules 2.a and 2.h (see slide 3).

## 3. Division C-only material (excluded from Division B)

These are not in the 2027 Div B rules:

- **Notes slides:** 25 (solid solution), 26 (phase diagrams), 27 (silicate tetrahedra structures), 28 (crystal systems), 49 (metamorphic facies), 50 (facies by setting), 53 (thin sections, explicitly Div C, State/National), and the Div C thin-section tip on 62.

## 5. Figure quality

- **36 figure files** were cropped from the native embedded page images (1275×1650 per page, the maximum resolution available) into `figures/`. Each was checked visually against the question it serves.
- **Low resolution at source:** the plate-boundary cross-section (C-Regional Q13–14, C-State Q18–20) has tiny labels. The items are still answerable from their stems.
- **Specimen photos whose identity rests on the stem:** the acicular specimen (B-State Q7, B-National Q61, called "actinolite"), zircon (C-State Q36) and turquoise (C-State Q25). The questions state the name. Don't reuse these photos as *identification* items without independent confirmation.
- **Reused photos:** the pyrite cubes, the vesicular lava, the cross-bedded sandstone, the serpentinite, the rhodochrosite and the Bowen diagram each appear in two or three exams. Captions are sometimes stale: the B-State Q22 photo is captioned "Image 2" although the stem says Image 4.
